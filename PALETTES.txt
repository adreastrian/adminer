Palette provenance

The catalog in js/palettes.js adapts familiar editor palettes to Adminer's semantic color roles. Surface steps and status colors are adjusted where needed for readable controls. It is not a verbatim terminal theme export.

Installed applications inspected on 2026-10-30:

Tern 0.9.4
  /Applications/Tern.app/Contents/MacOS/tern contains embedded named theme resources, including dark-catppuccin, light-catppuccin, dark-one, light-one, dark-monokai, dark-gruvbox, light-gruvbox, dark-tokyo-night, and light-tokyo-night.
  Base, foreground, and accent constants from those resources informed the corresponding families here. No application code or user configuration was copied.

Rex Beta 0.1.0
  /Applications/Rex Beta.app/Contents/MacOS/Rex contains separate lightThemeID and darkThemeID settings, independent light/dark palette selection strings, and Ghostty/iTerm theme import controls.
  Built-in names include Silver Point Light, Silver Point Dark, Charcoal Atelier, and Porcelain Morning. Assets.car color entries belong to the app icon. Exact Rex theme colors were not extracted or claimed.
  Rex's separate light/dark choices informed the two palette slots in Adminer.

Monokai Classic uses the familiar #272822 base, #f8f8f2 text, and #fd971f orange also present in Tern's dark-monokai resource. Paper is an Adminer light companion, not an official Monokai variant.

Ayu Light, Mirage, and Dark are role adaptations. Light accents and status colors are darkened for text contrast. All Catppuccin variants are available in either appearance slot. Frappé and Macchiato remain the default light-slot and dark-slot choices even though both palettes use dark colors.
